\begin{abstract}
We study simultaneous perturbation stochastic approximation (SPSA) with momentum for optimization under measurement noise. On a ten-dimensional noisy quadratic function, momentum $\beta = 0.6$ reduces the final squared error compared to classical SPSA at an equal measurement budget.
\end{abstract}
